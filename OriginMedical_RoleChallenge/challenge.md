# About Origin Medical

Origin Medical is a US-based artificial intelligence research laboratory building state-of-the-art AI systems to advance fetal medicine. Our mission is to democratize quality prenatal care for every expecting mother worldwide.

Globally affecting nearly 8 million babies each year, fetal anomalies are the leading cause of infant deaths in the first year of life (94% of them from middle and low-income nations), let alone babies with permanent physical and mental disabilities taking a very expensive toll on their families, community, and the nation. Even in developed nations like the United States of America, it is estimated that the total direct and indirect costs of the health care system are approximately $23 billion annually. Fetal anomalies and their effects, including stillbirths and neonatal deaths, can be prevented if screened early.

In the last decade, deep learning (DL), a set of biologically inspired AI algorithms capable of interpreting and analysing visual data (e.g., medical images), has been extensively developed and validated for a number of medical imaging applications. Origin Medical leverages the power of such proprietary DL algorithms to automatically screen for gross fetal anomalies from routine ultrasound scans by objectively analysing soft markers, fetal biometry, and anatomical landmarks similar to a fetal medicine expert, at the point of the examination itself.

# Role Challenge

**Role Challenge**

**Company Context**

MedAxis is a healthcare technology company offering software solutions and medical devices to hospitals, clinics, and diagnostic centres across the USA. The company is 4 years old, has ~120 employees, is Series A funded, and currently serves 60+ hospital clients across Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities.

Fast growth has created operational gaps in post-sales service. There is no standardised structure for how the company promises, delivers, and measures service after the contract is signed. Escalations come through informal channels; there are no clear response times or ownership, and three clients in the last quarter have formally flagged dissatisfaction. One is at churn risk.

Leadership has decided this must be fixed within 6 months through a post-sales service model designed, resourced, costed, operationalised, and capable of scaling to 150+ clients within 12 months. Your role is to design the blueprint, not to run day-to-day operations.

**Task 1: Strategic Diagnosis and Operating Model Design**

*Hints (for help, not limits):*

**Task 2: Cost, Resourcing, and 6-Month Plan**

*Hints (for help, not limits):*

* *Work within a total setup envelope of $30K–$40K over 6 months, with limited internal bandwidth; assume no external consulting firms.*
* *Think about what it would take to make your proposed model a reality: people, tools, process changes, and any other key enablers.*
* *Sketch a realistic 6-month path from today to a functioning, scalable post-sales model: how you would phase the work, what you see as critical milestones or decision points, and what major risks or dependencies you would flag to the management.*
