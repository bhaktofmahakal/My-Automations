# Open Gigantic — Founding AI Engineer Assignment III

## About the Role
This is a demanding role. It comes with a lot of ownership, high expectations, and a fast pace. If you enjoy solving hard problems and pushing yourself, you will likely enjoy it.

---

## About this Assignment
The assignment is intentionally designed with an open-ended approach. This is meant to give you the freedom to determine how you approach the problem and demonstrate how you operate in an environment with ambiguity and limited direction. 

Through this assignment, we want to understand how you navigate reasoning from first principles, structure your thinking, and make decisions when there is no clearly defined path and your decision matters most at that time. Since you will be leading this product after joining the team, we believe your approach to the assignment is just as important as the final outcome.

---

## 1) Understand the Product
Superbrain is built from three main parts:
* **IDE:** This is the layer you interact with, built as a fork of VS Code.
* **Agent:** The harness that carries out the work.
* **Architecture:** A proprietary context engine (**TokenFold**) that compresses and prioritizes code intelligence on the fly. It cuts token usage by 60 to 80 percent while keeping full awareness of the repository.

*Spend sufficient time exploring the product to develop a deep understanding of how its components interact and work together.*

---

## 2) Open-Ended Project
You will be asked to build a complex project that demonstrates your thought process, critical thinking, problem-solving skills, product thinking, and execution. 

There is no prescribed approach, technology stack, or specific product you need to build. You have the freedom to choose what you want to create. We are interested in seeing how you explore the problem, make decisions, prioritize what to build, and deliver a strong product within the given constraints.

**Examples suggested:**
* A live quiz or polling application
* An FPV game
* A real-time collaborative application

*Focus on building something meaningful that showcases how you think, solve problems, and turn an idea into a well-executed product.*

---

## 3) Product Strategy
* **A.** If you were building this product (Superbrain), what would you change or add next, and why?
* **B.** What major UI issues do you dislike, and how do you think they annoy current users?

---

## 4) Submission Requirements
Please include the following as part of your submission:
1. **What you built and why:** Briefly explain what you chose to build and the reasoning behind your choice.
2. **Architecture and design:** Explain the architecture, key design decisions, and why you chose your approach.
3. **GitHub repository:** Share the link to your GitHub repository.
4. **Deployment:** Share the deployed application link hosted on Vercel.
5. **Decision-making:** Explain the reasoning behind the key steps and decisions you made during the project. This will be evaluated in the next round.
6. **Single document:** Put all your answers and explanations in one document.
7. **Keep it authentic:** You can write your thoughts in raw form and use an LLM to improve the structure, but avoid over-polishing the content. We want to understand how you think, so keep your responses simple and in your own voice. Minor grammar mistakes are completely fine.
