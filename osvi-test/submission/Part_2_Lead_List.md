# Target Accounts & Outbound Strategy

## ICP

Beachhead: US multi-unit QSR franchise groups with 5–50 stores.

* **Revenue Density**: Mid-sized franchise footprints (averaging 27 stores across sourced leads) generate meaningful usage-based MRR ($1,000–$4,600/mo per group) while scaling cleanly.
* **Speed**: Founder/CEO/COO leads decisions in 2–4 weeks without enterprise procurement layers.
* **Direct Operational Pain**: Missed calls, catering inquiries, and labor shortages impact store P&L directly.
* **Simple Deployment**: Phone forwarding enables deployment before deep POS integrations.

## Account Scoring

| Dimension | Weight | Criteria |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **ICP Fit** | 30% | Confirmed 5–50 locations and multi-unit franchise status. |
| **Evidence Quality** | 25% | Verification via company site, franchisor profile, state filing, or vendor case study. |
| **Trigger Urgency** | 20% | Growth, divestiture, system migration, or documented operational pain. |
| **Buyer Accessibility** | 15% | Named owner, CEO, COO, or CFO publicly visible. |
| **Sales Speed** | 10% | Clear first outreach hook and 2–4 week pilot potential. |

---

## Scaling Workflow

1. Source accounts from franchisor directories, state business registries, vendor case studies, and industry news.
2. Filter for multi-unit franchise operators with 5–50 stores; exclude mega-chains (100+ stores) and single-unit owners.
3. Verify operational store counts via brand locators, official company domains, or published case studies.
4. Resolve decision-maker buying committees (Owner, CEO, COO, CFO, Dir of Ops) via domain email patterns, direct dials, and LinkedIn.
5. Sequence only accounts with verified multi-unit counts, direct decision-maker contact details, and clear operational scaling triggers.
