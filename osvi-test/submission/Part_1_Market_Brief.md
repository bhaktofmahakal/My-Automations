# US QSR Market Strategy

## Market Overview

The US Quick Service Restaurant (QSR) sector generates $423.7B annually within the $1.55T US foodservice market. Operators face rising wage inflation and labor turnover that make answering phone calls during peak hours difficult.

* **Wage Inflation**: Fast-food minimum wages in California reached $20.00/hr under AB 1228, while standard state minimum wages rose to $16.90/hr on Jan 1, 2026. Operators reduced store hours and staffing to protect 3%–5% margins.
* **Labor Turnover**: Restaurant employment remains 174,000 jobs below pre-pandemic levels, with annual turnover between 75% and 80%. Replacing a store worker costs $3,000 to $6,000.
* **Missed Call Frequency**: Locations miss 40% of incoming phone calls during peak dinner hours (5:00 PM – 8:00 PM).
* **Revenue Loss**: Industry estimates suggest the majority of callers who hit an unanswered line order from a competitor rather than leave a voicemail — QSR Magazine and Revmo's own data confirm the underlying $20B annual loss across the sector (up to $1,000/week or $52,000/year per store) and the 40% missed-call pattern that drives this behavior.

## Beachhead

Mid-size franchise groups (5–50 stores) are the primary target for launch:

* **Fast Sales Cycle**: Multi-unit owners manage local P&L directly and decide in 2–4 weeks without corporate procurement layers.
* **Contract Density**: An average 18-store account generates $21,600 ACV ($1,800/mo MRR).
* **Direct Pain**: At $52,000 lost per store annually from unanswered peak calls, an 18-store operator loses up to $936,000/year in uncaptured orders.
* **Low Friction**: 48-hour deployment via phone forwarding operates independently of corporate POS hardware.

## Key Objections

1. **"Will franchisors block third-party voice AI?"**  
   *Answer*: Franchisees own store P&L and phone lines. Osvi forwards unanswered calls using standard PSTN/SIP forwarding without modifying corporate POS hardware.

2. **"What if competitors drop prices?"**  
   *Answer*: Competitors with fixed monthly fees ($399–$600/store) carry high fixed overhead. Osvi's usage pricing (~$100/store) maintains gross margin while scaling cleanly.

3. **"How does 48-hour deployment work?"**  
   *Answer*: Onboarding takes three steps: (1) Upload store menu PDF, (2) Forward unanswered calls after 3 rings, (3) Run test calls. Total time required: under 2 hours per group.
