Vocallabs is an **infrastructure startup** selling white-label Voice AI (VocalStack, Vocalassist, Vocallabs Identity, MCP Server, VocalFlow, PocoDisk).
- To sell or support a white-label voice engine, you cannot be a pure marketer. You must understand end-to-end audio latency (STT -> LLM -> TTS), SIP trunking, WebRTC packet jitter, and function calling.
- I bridge the gap between engineering (talking to your AI voice architects) and revenue (talking to BPO owners about gross margin arbitrage).

### Pillar 3: Extreme Ownership & Founder Mindset

- I view every problem through unit economics, partner margin, and system reliability.
