⚡ DM Response Strategy

  • First Response < 5 min — AI auto-acknowledges every DM immediately
  • Hot Lead Alerts — Sales team gets instant Slack + WhatsApp notification with full conversation context
  • Context Preservation — AI reads the entire conversation thread, not just the last message
  • Handoff Protocol — When AI can't handle, it smoothly hands off to human: "Aapko humare team member se connect kar rahe hain 🙏"
  • Follow-up Tracking — If no human response in 30 min, re-alert the team
  • After-hours Auto-reply — "Namaste! Humari team kal subah 9 baje aapko reply karegi 🙏"

Daily Operating Procedure (SOP)

The complete daily workflow for the social media team

🌅 9:00 AM

Morning Review

Check overnight auto-report in Slack. Review AI-flagged items that need human attention. Respond to any pending escalations. Check unresolved complaints from yesterday.

📱 9-12 PM

Peak Hours — Active Monitoring

AI runs every 5 min. Team monitors Slack for real-time alerts. Respond to hot leads within 15 min. Handle escalated complaints. Process sales inquiries.

🍽️ 12-1 PM

Lunch Break — AI Takes Over

AI continues auto-processing. Only critical complaints trigger phone alerts. Team member on rotation for urgent issues.

📱 1-5 PM

Afternoon Session

Continue monitoring. Review pending items. Follow up on morning leads. Update customer complaint statuses. Moderate new post comments.

📊 5-6 PM

End-of-Day Wrap Up

Review daily dashboard. Ensure all complaints have at least L1 response. Hand over pending items to next shift or AI. Clear Slack backlogs.

🤖 6 PM

Auto Daily Report

n8n generates and sends daily report to Slack + Email. Includes all KPIs, category breakdown, pending items, and AI-generated insights.

🌙 After Hours

AI Continues Running

AI auto-replies with "After hours" template. Spam continues to be cleaned. Critical complaints (detected by keywords like "legal", "media") trigger phone alert to manager.

Challenges & Solutions

Anticipated problems and how we handle them

⚠️ Challenge 1: AI Misclassification

Problem: AI might classify a genuine complaint as positive feedback or miss sarcasm in Hindi comments.

Solution:

  • Add a "confidence score" to AI output — if below 70%, flag for human review
  • Weekly AI audit: sample 50 random classifications, check accuracy
  • Continuous prompt improvement with misclassified examples
  • Maintain a "feedback loop" sheet where team marks corrections

⚠️ Challenge 2: Meta API Rate Limits

Problem: Meta Graph API has rate limits — too many requests can get throttled.

Solution:

  • Batch requests — fetch all comments in one call, not individually
  • Cache processed comment IDs to avoid re-processing
  • Implement exponential backoff on API errors
  • Use webhook-based triggers where possible (Meta Webhooks)

⚠️ Challenge 3: Hindi/Hinglish Understanding

Problem: Comments in mixed Hindi/English/slang might confuse the AI.

Solution:

  • Use models good at Hindi (Llama 3.1, Gemma 2 have strong multilingual support)
  • Include Hindi examples in the classification prompt
  • Build a "common phrases" dictionary for Divine Hindu context
  • Test with real Divine Hindu comments during setup

⚠️ Challenge 4: Negative Viral Comments

Problem: A complaint goes viral before team notices.

Solution:

  • AI detects "high engagement" complaints (many likes/replies on a comment)
  • Sentiment-based alerting — multiple negative comments on same post = alert
  • Instant L3 escalation for comments with 5+ replies
  • Pre-approved crisis response templates ready for quick deployment
