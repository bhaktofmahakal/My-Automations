## Ada Chen Rekhi

**Insight:** To receive actionable, contextual advice, structure your requests to minimize the responder's cognitive burden while providing specific options for them to evaluate.

**Tactical advice:**
- Structure requests as frictionless reactions to a specific list of options to minimize the advisor's cognitive load.
- Use unbiased, rationale-seeking questions to ensure feedback is rooted in logic rather than a desire to please.
- Select a mix of subject matter experts and peers who know your personal style to ensure feedback is both technically and personally contextual.
