## Christine Itwaru

**Insight:** Product operations acts as a critical bridge between development and revenue teams, ensuring transparency and alignment during periods of rapid growth.

**Tactical advice:**
- Establish standard communication loops that give sales and support teams visibility into the product roadmap.
- Create a dedicated function to manage stakeholder expectations to prevent PMs from becoming bottlenecks for information.
- Ensure the business is operationally ready for new features by coordinating across sales and marketing ops teams.

## Gustav Söderström

**Insight:** Major product redesigns are often necessitated by fundamental shifts in underlying technology, such as the transition from user curation to algorithmic recommendation.

**Tactical advice:**
- Rethink the entire user experience when a new technological paradigm (like generative AI) makes the previous interface logic obsolete.
- Build high-production-value prototypes to test how fundamental business model changes affect the redesigned user experience.
- Empathize with the creator side of the platform by using your own creation tools to identify friction points before a major launch.

*Source: Gustav Söderström @ 00:44:10*

## Sachin Monga

**Insight:** Piloting features with a small, engaged group of power users ensures that broad rollouts are refined and genuinely solve customer problems.

**Tactical advice:**
- Create a dedicated program to test new product concepts with a select group of core users before broad release.
- Iterate on feature design based on qualitative feedback from power users during the pilot phase.
- Gradually expand access to new features to monitor performance and impact at scale.

## Tamar Yehoshua

**Insight:** Product decisions should be based on the needs of the majority rather than the reactions of a vocal minority.
