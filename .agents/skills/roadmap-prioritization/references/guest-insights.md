**Insight:** Product roadmaps should shift from rigid plans limited by manual effort toward curating and directing organic growth patterns.

## Lenny Rachitsky

**Insight:** By implementing a multi-stage prioritization process that moves from quick T-shirt sizing to detailed investigation, teams can double their business impact without increasing development speed.

**Tactical advice:**
- Gather a backlog of at least 5x the number of ideas you can realistically build in a quarter.
- Apply the RICE framework (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort) for an initial high-level ranking.
- Refine the top candidates from your RICE shortlist using the more rigorous DRICE (Detailed RICE) framework.

*Source: [Introducing DRICE: a modern prioritization framework](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/introducing-drice-a-modern-prioritization-framework)*

---

> "In short, RICE-ing is the process of T-shirt scoring (i.e. S/M/L) your ideas according to their: Reach: How many of your customers would experience the new idea; Impact: If the idea pans out, how much it would affect conversion; Confidence: How likely it is to work; Effort: How much time it would take to validate (from the engineering team)."

**Insight:** Initial prioritization should use directional 'SWAGs' and relative T-shirt sizing to quickly compare dozens of ideas without getting bogged down in false precision.

**Tactical advice:**
- Score each idea on Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort using S/M/L values.
- Calculate a numerical score using the formula: (Reach % * Impact % * Confidence %) / Weeks of effort.
- Sort the final list and select a shortlist that represents about 2x your team's quarterly capacity.

*Source: [Introducing DRICE: a modern prioritization framework](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/introducing-drice-a-modern-prioritization-framework)*

---

> "During DRICE, we will go from: A 30-second estimate to a 30-minute estimate; A relative scoring (S/M/L) to a $X of expected annualized revenue; 'Wouldn’t it be cool if' to 'We are shovel-ready'."

**Insight:** Moving from relative T-shirt sizes to concrete financial models and day-level engineering tasks ensures projects are 'shovel-ready' and truly high-ROI before work begins.

**Tactical advice:**
- Write a clear hypothesis that anybody without context can understand.
- Build a bottom-up financial model to estimate the specific incremental annualized revenue.
- Engage a growth engineer to 'kick the tires' on assumptions and identify potential technical shortcuts.

*Source: [Introducing DRICE: a modern prioritization framework](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/introducing-drice-a-modern-prioritization-framework)*
