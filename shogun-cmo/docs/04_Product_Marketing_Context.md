# Product Marketing Context
**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-08-13

## Product Overview
**ShogunCMO** is an AI-native personal marketing operating system built by Select, Inc. Unlike traditional AI chatbots or even autonomous agent platforms that require heavy manual calibration, ShogunCMO acts as a "Personal AGI" for marketing. It leverages a continuous, "live memory" of the user's workday—passively observing activity across apps like Slack, Notion, GitHub, and Figma. Running locally on macOS for privacy, it uses this deeply ingrained context to proactively execute go-to-market tasks, draft launch assets, and orchestrate campaigns without the user ever having to set the context.

## Target Audience
- **Primary:** Early-stage technical founders, solo founders, and indie hackers (particularly within the Y Combinator and Silicon Valley ecosystems).
- **Secondary:** Knowledge workers, growth engineers, and lean product teams who move fast and suffer from context fragmentation across multiple siloed tools.

## Problems & Pain Points
- **Context Fragmentation:** Founders constantly context-switch between coding, managing, and marketing. Marketing efforts suffer because transferring product knowledge to external marketers (or AI tools) is exhausting.
- **The "Amnesia" Problem:** Current LLMs (like ChatGPT or Claude) forget context between sessions, requiring founders to write long prompts repeatedly.
- **Manual Calibration:** Autonomous tools (like Okara) require extensive upfront setup, URL scraping, and manual tuning to get the brand voice right.
- **Privacy Concerns:** Founders are hesitant to feed sensitive, unreleased product roadmaps and source code into cloud-based AI systems.

## Competitive Landscape
- **Traditional Conversational AI:** ChatGPT, Claude (Require manual prompting, suffer from amnesia).
- **Autonomous Marketing Agents:** Okara.ai, CrewClaw (Require static onboarding, lack live workspace memory).
- **Memory/Search Tools:** Rewind, Recall (Focus on passive search and recall, lacking the proactive "action layer" to execute marketing tasks).

## Differentiation
- **Memory-First Action Layer:** ShogunCMO doesn't just remember what you did; it acts on it. It drafts the Product Hunt launch post because it saw you merge the final PR.
- **Zero-Friction Context:** No more writing prompts. The AI knows the brand voice, the product specs, and the target audience because it observes the founders' actual work.
- **Local macOS Privacy:** The memory is captured and stored locally. Proprietary data never leaves the device.
- **Proactive Execution:** It tees up the next marketing step before the founder even asks for it.

## Objections
- *“Will this slow down my Mac?”* (Requires clear messaging around optimized local performance and efficient background processing).
- *“Is it creepy that it watches everything?”* (Overcome by emphasizing the strict local-only, privacy-first architecture).
- *“I don’t want it publishing without my permission.”* (Clarify that it tees up actions and drafts for approval before executing).

## Switching Dynamics
- **Triggers:** A founder getting overwhelmed prepping for a Product Hunt launch, feeling the pain of writing repetitive update emails, or getting frustrated trying to teach ChatGPT their brand voice for the 10th time.
- **Friction:** Installing an app that requires deep OS permissions (Screen Recording/Accessibility) requires a high degree of trust.
